REFURBISHED DESKTOP COMPUTER WARRANTY (90-Day Limited Warranty - Parts and Labor)

  • The refurbished PC is covered by a 90-day limited warranty starting from the date of delivery to the customer.
  • PARTS AND LABOR COVERAGE: If any hardware component fails due to defects in materials or workmanship within the warranty period, we will repair or replace the defective part at no cost to the buyer.
  • EQUIVALENT REPLACEMENT: In cases where an identical replacement part is not available, Muda PCs will replace the defective part with one of equal or comparable specifications and performance.
  • LABOR COSTS: All labor required for diagnosis, repair, or replacement of defective components is covered during the 90-day warranty period.
  • EXCLUSIONS: This warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, neglect, unauthorized modifications, other warranty/RMA, software-related issues, data loss, or recovery. Unauthorized modifications to include overclocking of any kind (cpu, ram, or graphics card), replacing components, undervolting, software installation, or BIOS updating. Impacts such as fluids, fire, flood, wind, earthquake, lightning or similar disasters are also excluded from warranty. No reseller, agent or employee is authorized to make any modification, extension or addition to this warranty. This warranty extends only to products purchased directly from MudaPCs.com and is effective only if the products are purchased for use within the continental U.S. and contiguous 48-states (not inclusive of Hawaii and Alaska).
  • ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS: In the event that your computer system or component is returned to Muda PCs LLC for repair or replacement, should no defect in materials or workmanship be found, or if no problem found as reported, you may be subject to return shipping. No compensation or warranty extensions will be granted for normal warranty repairs or replacements. No compensation or warranty extensions will be granted for any delays in returning a repaired computer or replacement parts. Muda PCs LLC accepts no responsibility for any software programs, data or information stored on any media or any parts of any products returned for repair to Muda PCs LLC. Products with modification to the serial number and/or factory identification labels whether removed, relocated, falsified, defaced, damaged, altered or made illegible. The use of inadequate shipment packaging or use of inadequate packing material resulting in damages to the product while in transit back to Muda PCs LLC will void warranty. We highly recommend using packaging that the system came with to avoid damages. We will not accept your own parts being sent to us to be used in the event of a repair. You are responsible for shipping charges to and from Muda PCs LLC. Products that are rendered non-functional due to roach or other infestation are NOT covered in warranty. In such cases when an infestation is discovered, all action will cease, the computer will immediately be sealed with a plastic bag, and the computer or component will be returned to the customer as-is.
