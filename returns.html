Refunds and Returns

At Muda PCs, we pride ourselves on offering high-quality computers at highly competitive prices. Once an order is placed and the PC has been shipped, processing return requests due to a change of mind requires considerable time and staff resources—time that could be better spent serving other customers. We kindly ask that you ensure you are fully committed to your purchase before completing checkout. Please take a moment to review the system specifications and confirm that the PC meets your needs. We want every customer to be satisfied and confident in their decision to game with a quality Muda PC.

At Muda PCs, we offer a 14-day return window from the date your order is delivered. Customers must initiate and complete the return within 14 calendar days from the date the shipping carrier marks the item as delivered to the address provided at checkout. To qualify for a return, the item must be in the exact condition in which it was received—unopened, unused, and in its original packaging as shipped by Muda PCs. A valid proof of purchase is also required for all returns. If you have changed your mind and wish to request a return and refund, you must contact us at mudapcsassistance@gmail.com within the 14-day return window noted above. In your email, please include your order number and the reason for your return request.

If Muda PCs agrees in writing to accept your return, you will be responsible for covering the return shipping cost. We require the use of the same reliable shipping service originally used to deliver your order to ensure the product is returned safely and securely. We will provide you with the shipping cost details and a payment link. In addition to shipping costs, you will also be required to reimburse:

  • The online payment processing fee charged to us by our payment provider at the time of your original purchase.
  • A $150 Inspection and Reshelving Fee, which covers the time and resources needed to inspect, verify, and restock the returned item.

These charges must be paid in full prior to the return being processed. Once we receive your payment, we will issue a prepaid return shipping label along with detailed instructions on how and where to return your package. After we receive the returned item, our team will conduct a thorough inspection and testing of the system. If it is determined that the computer has been opened, used, or is not in its original condition, no refund will be issued.

Please note:

  • Returns will not be accepted without prior written approval from Muda PCs.
  • Items sent back without a return authorization will not be accepted or refunded.

Refunds

Once we have received and inspected your returned item, we will notify you in writing whether a full or partial refund has been approved. If a full refund is approved, it will be issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. If a partial refund is approved (due to applicable fees or the condition of the returned item), the partial amount will also be issued to your original payment method within 10 business days.

While we process refunds within this timeframe, your bank or credit card provider may take additional time to post the funds to your account. If more than 15 days have passed since we confirmed the refund on our end and you have not received it, please contact us at mudapcsassistance@gmail.com so we can assist you further.
